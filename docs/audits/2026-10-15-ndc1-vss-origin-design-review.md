For the smallest candidate, select the system-provider `CLIENT_ACCESSIBLE` branch explicitly and keep retention/exposure/security/host support as named qualification conditions; do not infer persistence or indefinite existence from read-only status. If relevant state changes or a root is omitted, refuse. The report already recognizes independently proved quiescence; make that the concrete condition rather than leaving exact cross-volume simultaneity as an apparent new prerequisite.

The requester, VSS service/provider and shadow storage are separate mutable owners; their effects, capacity, lifetime, access and release custody must be declared. “Read-only origin” does not mean no host writes or automatic original-namespace nonmutation.
