So the proposed amendment "make 17-19 depend on H + I4-equivalent" is not a reordering; it rewrites P2's release predicate, P2C's behaviour and the protected plan's Blocked-by lines.
2. **"I1, I2, I3 deferrable to L2".** They cannot be deferred past P2CG2, P2CH or P2C: `P2CG2.predecessors = [P2CG1I4]`, `P2CG1I4.predecessors = [P2CG1I3]`, and so on down to H. 3. **"P2CG2, P2CH deferrable unless proven otherwise".** Proven otherwise: both are P2C predecessors and P2C is Task 17. P2CH additionally owns the very spec Task 18 extends; running P2 before P2CH would either violate P2CH's ownership or force P2CH to re-prove on a moved target.
4. **"Register a minimal fail-closed guard for L1, keep I4 for L2".** A new unit, with leaves identical to I4's (`coopMissionCreate.ts`, `coopMissionCreate.test.ts`, refusals test, harness). That is a registration change (new holder, new restriction entries in roadmap.json), not docs-only, and it would be refused at admission for overlap with I4. Use I4.
