So an application refusal would indeed gate GM controls today; P2CF2's "reason starts with campaign-application-" filter at both sites, before the existing `setLastRefusalCode`, is the correct fix, and the optimistic clear on `CampaignEvent` needs no change. `fail(...)` for INTERNAL_ERROR variants sends Error then Close and closes the socket (2136-2151), reaching the consumer as a close, as the receipt says.
