### LSC-0 prototype and fit

Four delta snippets under .lsc/specs target the existing design and three registered delta-spec paths. Two registered spec files do not yet exist and are deliberately treated as additions, not absent permission. The snippets specify operator-managed trusted same-host seats, primary-vault separation, one-password-per-mint, lockout, public metadata, bounded bearer lifetime, idempotent operation IDs, re-provision rotation, reload/reconnect and password-prompted expiry recovery. Root adoption is distinguished from pending direct owner answers.

Revocation does not invalidate signatures or force disconnects: old bearer works until original expiry; subsequent mint/renewal fails. Reprovision creates a new keypair/playerId and requires normal membership admission again. Seat selection grants no membership or GM role. B15 and PRIV-1 holds remain. Fit **plausible as registered** at 9 files and a 115-line conservative sketch; final appended design/spec/lifecycle diff and authentic evidence must be measured before admission. The policy mutation fixture is research support, not a newly authorized tenth product leaf.

### LSC-1 prototype and fit

Repository creates an independent table, persists encrypted credential payload/generation/revocation/failures/lock deadline/unique operation ID, uses BEGIN IMMEDIATE and expected-generation CAS, and reopens durable guest keys without touching vault_identities. Service reuses createIdentity/unlockIdentity/signData/canonicalTokenPayload/derivePlayerId and ordinary wire encoding. Primary operator password verification is injected; production must wire real getActive/unlockIdentity, rate limiting and loopback request defenses, never trust a caller boolean or seat ID.

Mint: bounded password input, aggregate 12-attempt/minute pre-KDF window (including guessed IDs), durable three-failure/30-second seat lockout, one in-flight mint per seat, and revocation/generation rechecks after KDF and signing. These numbers are prototype policy proposals, not owner answers. Loopback callers share an aggregate window; denial of service by the trusted operator/shared OS remains possible. Every mint requires the password; no durable unlock session is introduced. Concurrent same-seat mint is rejected/throttled and can be retried; simultaneous provisions use SQL CAS/idempotency.

The prototype's lazy CREATE TABLE matches the existing IdentityRepository precedent, but a durable new authentication namespace and lockout state deserve the central migration runner's upgrade/retry/transaction proof. Choose the explicit migration path rather than treating an untested lazy schema as a way to fit. Three required schema leaves turn the 14-file candidate into **17 files**, and two are outside LSC-1 ownership. Root N2 therefore requires a separately registered schema predecessor, not permission to consume a spare slot. The sketch does not prove primary-vault authorization routes, cross-worker locking, actual HTTP CSRF/origin defenses or complete request-body idempotency semantics. A repeated operation ID with different parameters currently returns the old public row; decide/document whether this is accepted retry semantics or a 409 before product implementation.

### LSC-2 prototype and fit

LocalSeatCredentialStore persists only selected public seat metadata and an ordinary bearer in the supplied per-profile session store. Wrong-seat/expired/malformed data is cleared; failed renewal clears the previous bearer; selection changes during an awaited mint cannot store another seat's result. The scratch guest panel prompts again, clears password React state after submit, and offers forgetting/recovery with trust disclosure. It has not been mounted or rendered in a browser; its JSX is not full UI acceptance.

The HTTP/WS harness is an integration prototype on one real loopback listener. It executes unchanged product mint for GM, scratch mint for two seats, production Ed25519 verifier at reconnect, independent profile-storage facsimiles, public IDs, reload reconstruction, renewal, expiry and disk SQLite reopen. Admission roles come from an explicit fixture membership map, **not** the seat selector. It does not invoke CampaignHostRegistry/bindCampaignSyncConnection or prove authoritative campaign actions, owner law, privacy projections or packaged Electron.

Packaged proof needs a config that attaches to the actual installed instance. The default playwright.config.ts has E2E global setup, per-run readiness and webServer configured with E2E/durable-store env; a normal invocation risks a second dev server or identity-fixture path. Draft .lsc/playwright.local-seat-packaged.config.ts explicitly has no webServer/globalSetup and fixed 127.0.0.1:3001, serial workers, original 30-second timeout. This extra registration leaf triggers N2 even though it would bring the old 14-file list to 15. Move the packaged spec and release-proof document into LSC-2P rather than squeezing them or silently changing package.json. No npm registration is currently needed; if needed later, it is another separately frozen literal.
