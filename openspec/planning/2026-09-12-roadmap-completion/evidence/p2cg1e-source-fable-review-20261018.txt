## 5. Per-class findings

- **Authority.** Both doors key on the installed port alone; the view is built per call in the API graph and `revalidate` refuses any other handle or a non-transactional call (`context-mismatch`), so a foreign entry's view cannot be grafted (pinned `entryView: false`, `ownHandle: true`). No view fails closed on both doors. The receipt preflight is read-only (durable census and match census unchanged across every refusal and the accepted call) and carries no principal, exactly as the legacy gate did; the only mutating door binds the authenticated player to session membership. Residual: RM-E2 shows no case distinguishes "port installed" from "entry with a launch view exists", which coincide in every fixture; the product is right, the pin is missing (CF-F1).
- **Privacy.** Receipt-mode refusals are code-only: `participants-behind` loses its `participantIds`, the legacy `requiredRevision`/`behind` rows stay on the legacy branch, hydration is never serialised; the wire-string pin and RM-E7 hold this. The transport adds nothing to the wire except the non-secret remembered `lastSeq` on bound joins, which no shipped client sends.
- **Replay.** Head-drift re-sends are the exact `sentAck` bytes (P2CA answers `duplicate`); a stale or foreign confirmation does not confirm (`appliedView` JSON equality, pinned); duplicate offers are byte-compared (unchanged); the CF7 ignore grants no confirmation and sends no ACK. Residual: the pre-ACK unknown-receipt case (RM-E4) is unpinned; the product recovers, which is the conservative answer.
- **Idempotency.** `recover` is reached only behind `!failed` guards (message path, timer, `onclose`, `drain` call sites), the CF12 signal fires once across repeated `onclose`/`onerror` (pinned), and the confirmation timer is one-shot and nulled before it runs. Residual: a remote close after a prior recovery is unpinned (RM-E5); the product's `!failed` guard keeps it to one signal.
- **Concurrency.** Prepare runs before the read transaction and revalidate inside it on the same handle (order probe pinned); three injected drifts between the two are refused before materialization with censuses unchanged; the create path keeps C IMMEDIATE then M with re-admission before C (RM-E3); the transport's counter and timer belong to `sent` only, a newer pending offer neither fires nor hides the wait, and no late callback acts after close, recovery or confirmation (pinned with fake timers). DoS judgment: a server can provoke at most 4 identical ACK attempts per sent ACK (1 + 3 re-sends, no backoff, each a short P2CA transaction) and the client waits at most one 60 s window per ACK before giving up to the consumer; nothing retries on arbitrary failure. Acceptable and bounded; the absence of backoff is noted (M2).
