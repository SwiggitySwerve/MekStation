### Disposition

**Clause-correction assigned to U99**, not a direct triage edit. The accepted actors are existing event authors, not newly invented roles:

- `authorPlayerId === 'migration'`: never deliver a full-state baseline to a restricted viewer;
- system-authored genesis: may establish the viewer's initial authorized fold if no earlier fact was withheld;
- any full-state checkpoint after the viewer's fold withheld a fact (including a refused baseline): withhold and keep the latch;
- a viewer entitled to all relevant scopes (host/owner GM): receives the baseline;
- admitted incremental facts continue to fold after the latch.

Precise future ADDED requirement in `openspec/changes/harden-gm-two-player-campaign-sessions/specs/gm-authority-redaction/spec.md`:

```md
### Requirement: Full-State Campaign Baselines Obey Author and Prior-Withhold Law
A full-state campaign baseline SHALL be offered only to an authorized viewer. A migration-authored baseline SHALL never be serialized to a viewer lacking all scopes represented by that state. A system-authored genesis MAY initialize a restricted viewer only before that viewer's authorized fold has withheld any fact. Once a fact or baseline has been withheld, every later full-state checkpoint SHALL remain withheld for that viewer; admitted incremental facts SHALL continue in authorized delivery order. A host or owner GM entitled to every represented scope SHALL retain the full baseline.
```

### Owner packet: `PK-triage-checkpoint-day-effects`

**Closed question: Must every non-day effect produced by co-op AdvanceDay be committed as independently replayable journal events, or may those effects remain authoritative only through the adopted whole-record checkpoint?**

| Option | Consequence | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| A. Independent events (recommended) | Matches the active design delta's "every committed mutation" and exact replay law; requires event coverage for all day-effect families and migration/replay/privacy tests | High |
| B. Explicit checkpoint carriage | Smaller implementation; requires a product-spec correction admitting checkpoint-carried authority and defining replay/recovery/visibility semantics, weakening the current event-completeness architecture but not privacy | Medium |
| C. Narrow AdvanceDay to day count only | Avoids checkpoint authority but removes or postpones existing day side effects in co-op | Medium/high product change |

Agent recommendation: **A**, because the active authority delta already makes the journal the source of truth and calls snapshots derived materializations, not mutation authority (`design.../coop-campaign-sync/spec.md:78-99`).  
Revert cost: A adds event/schema/migration surface and is expensive to reverse after histories exist; B is cheap to reverse before archive but creates spec debt; C changes user-visible campaign progression and has the highest behavioral revert cost.
