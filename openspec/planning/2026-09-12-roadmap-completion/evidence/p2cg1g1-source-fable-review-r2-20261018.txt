## 2. The cut design, class by class

Mechanics at the head: `settling` counts offers an active binding has consumed whose step chain has not finished (incremented synchronously at consume, decremented in a trailing `.then` after `settle` resolves or `rejoin` ran). `dispose` adds the transport to a module `WeakSet` when `live && settling > 0`. `bind` on an active binding calls `rejoin()` at once when the transport is in the set (after the quiet-window decision; `rejoin` clears the window). `rejoin` closes the transport, which unregisters it, so the next effect run connects a fresh transport that is not in the set.

- **Authority.** A cut can only cause a rejoin; it never advances a receipt. The disposed binding still confirms nothing (every `settle` path re-checks `live` before `confirm`), the remount on a cut transport confirms nothing on the dead chain (PROBE-A: confirms `[v1-r0]`, acks `[v1-r0]`), and the replacement view starts from EMPTY and is applied and ACKed on its own socket (PROBE-S3: hydration input EMPTY, acks `[v2-r0]`, balance 600000). The direction of every residual is unchanged from round 1: ACKs withheld or restarted, never advanced.
- **Privacy.** The cut set holds transport identities only (WeakSet, GC-bound, never serialised); the rejoin sends the same `CampaignJoin` F sends on a fresh socket (host still quotes -1, omitted). Nothing new reaches the wire. The receipt's claim of no wire change holds.
- **Replay.** Receipt order is preserved: the cut chain is abandoned whole (the transport is closed with its pending list), and the replacement adopts its baseline at -1 and restarts the chain. The cut marking does not touch `heardAt`, so CF-G3's window semantics are unchanged (the window decision is made before the cut check; rejoin clears whatever was armed). Against a legacy server the surface folds campaign frames synchronously and nothing settles, so a bound legacy binding is never cut (by construction; RM-G9-rerun still survives, see CF-H6).
- **Idempotency.** Double dispose: the second sees `live === false` and marks nothing. StrictMode mount/cleanup/mount: no offer has been consumed at mount, so `settling` is 0 and nothing is cut. A cut transport bound twice: the first active bind rejoins and closes it; the stored connect never hands out an unregistered transport, so a second active bind cannot see it (PROBE-S4: a second remount while the held refresh is still pending rejoins at once, the third remount binds the replacement and is not cut). The set is never cleared, but every member is a closed transport reachable only through `held.transport` until the next host claim (CF-H13, unchanged).
- **Concurrency.** `settling` is mutated on task boundaries only (consume runs inside the transport's synchronous dispatch; the decrement is a microtask after the step). A dispose is a React cleanup, i.e. a task or a sync-lane microtask flush, so the only interleaving hazard is a dispose scheduled as a microtask between a successful `confirm` and the trailing decrement. That is the spurious-fire class below. The "miss" direction is closed: the predicate is evaluated at dispose, the increment precedes any yield, and R2-M3 (release the count when the step starts) is caught by all three CF-H1 rows.

**Can it fire spuriously?** Healthy settle: no (the decrement microtasks drain before any task can dispose). Dispose during the awaited refresh: correct fire (the resumed step returns on `!live`). Same-tick dispose after consume, before the step starts: correct fire (PROBE-S2: refresh never called, acks withheld at `[v1-r0]`, the remount rejoins). The one spurious class: a dispose in a microtask after `confirm` returned and before the trailing decrement; PROBE-S1 hooks `confirmApplied` to unmount in `Promise.resolve().then` once v1-r1 is confirmed, and the remount rejoins (socket 0 closed) although `acks(0) == [v1-r0, v1-r1]`. In production this ordering needs a React update committed during `project()`/`confirm()` whose sync-lane flush unmounts the surface; the dashboard mount is unconditional, the shell mount keys on the route, `projectAuthoritativeStateOntoCampaign` spreads the campaign and preserves `coopSession` (so the effect deps `campaignId/coopMode/roomCode/matchId` are stable), and `store` is the singleton StoreApi. Not reachable today; latent for any future mount gated on projected state. Hardening (one-line class): release the count synchronously at the point the step can no longer confirm or has confirmed (e.g. decrement inside `settle` immediately before `confirm` and on each early return, or keep a precise "consumed and unconfirmed" count) and drop the trailing `.then`. Pin: PROBE-S1's shape (CF-H14).

**Can it miss?** No ordering found. Dispose before the step starts, during the await, or after a failed confirm are all covered (the last rejoins on its own). An inactive binding (`!active`) returns `isOffer(message)` without settling, so an offer it swallows is unconfirmed and uncut; that is F-era code on a transport bound to a different campaign than the mounted one, which the host connect cannot currently refuse because it passes no `expectedCampaignId` (CF-H16, a G2 design requirement, not a G1 regression).

**Interactions.** CF-G3 cursor-record skip: unaffected (cut check follows the window decision; both gap rows still pass; the AM1/AM3/RM-G5/RM-G6 classes are untouched). F's window: a cut remount skips straight to rejoin; the replacement arms its own window as every fresh transport does, so when the server stays quiet the CF-G4 cycle continues (PROBE-A and PROBE-S4 reach three sockets by 2T; CF-H7 unchanged). Replacement transports: new objects, never in the set (PROBE-S3). Memory: WeakSet plus one integer per binding closure; the strong reference that keeps a cut transport alive is the store's `held.transport`, bounded to one (CF-H13).

**Display at a cut remount (PROBE-RP).** `claimCampaignHostFold` runs before `bindApplication`, so a cut remount is `continued` and re-projects the retained fold (750000: the cut offer's state, which the disposed binding folded before its await) for the instant before the rejoin's replacement baseline lands. The server published that offer, so the value shown is the server's state; display-only, transient, nothing certified (CF-H17, optional).

## 3. CF-H3 "not behind" soundness

`continues` now requires `lineage.cell.current.lastSequence >= transport.lastSeq()`. The transport cursor moves on every `CampaignSnapshot`/`CampaignEvent` with a wire event (`Math.max`), whether or not the fold accepted it. The fold's cursor moves only on accepted frames and can be adopted ahead of the wire sequence (a legacy baseline at `sequence: -1` adopts at 0 while the cursor stays -1; a sequenced snapshot adopts at `payload.revision` when present).

- **Behind (the CF-H3 case).** A frame during an unmount gap moves the cursor past the lineage: not continued, no re-show, fresh cell (pinned; R2-M7 off-by-one caught by that row; MH3 caught).
- **Ahead.** Only reachable through frames the fold's own ordering guard would reject (`foldCampaignEvent` refuses `sequence <= lastSequence`; `foldCampaignSnapshot` refuses an older adoption). PROBE-H3a: a snapshot at wire sequence 2 with revision 10 adopts at 10 (cursor 2); a gap frame at 7 leaves it continued (re-show 640000, correct: the fold would have rejected 7); a gap frame at 11 makes it fresh (loaded record 1000000). Equality would refuse every legacy-baseline lineage (the control row; MH4 and R2-M6 caught), so "not behind" is the right predicate. I found no lineage ahead of the transport that should not continue.
- **Conflation (note, not a defect).** A sequenced frame the fold rejected while mounted (PROBE-H3b: an unparseable snapshot at sequence 3 on a bound legacy stream) also leaves the lineage "behind", so the next remount gets a fresh cell and no re-show (balance 1000000 instead of the held 640000). Safe-side and identical to pre-G1 behaviour (every bind started at EMPTY); in receipt mode such a frame is `fold-rejected` and already forces recovery. Recorded under CF-H3's closure, no action.

16. **CF-H16 (new; bind only a transport bound to the mounted campaign) [P2CG1G2, which owns the host connect call].** The host `connectStoredCampaignSyncTransport` call passes no `expectedCampaignId`, so a stored transport bound to another campaign on the same match would be bound inactive, and an inactive binding swallows offers (`return isOffer(message)`) that nothing confirms, cuts or hears.
