## P2CG1HP: prototype, proof, and fit

### Ruled policy implemented

The hook holds one mounted recovery episode keyed by **campaignId / matchId / playerId**, not by socket identity. Replacement transports consume the same **two automatic quiet-window retries**. Initial connection plus two replacements is three sockets total; the next quiet deadline closes the third, unsubscribes recovery/confirmation listeners, cancels its quiet timer, and does not increment rejoins.

A validated `transport.onAppliedViewConfirmed` callback is the healthy reset. Transport parsing already checks match/player/campaign/offer equality before invoking it. Sending an ACK, receiving a heartbeat or arbitrary frame, or binding a replacement does not reset the episode. Explicit reload/remount starts a fresh hook instance and is the offered retry action; identity change starts that seat's new episode. No durable cross-route budget, new store or additional retry control is claimed.

Exhaustion renders an inline paragraph with `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"`, exactly:

> Campaign sync could not connect. Reload this campaign to try again.

It uses the existing connected surface, with no new UI component. A new identity clears that prior episode's visible error. Late callbacks from closed/disposed transports cannot reopen the exhausted episode. Ordinary non-quiet recovery signals retain their existing semantics; this policy bounds the specifically registered quiet-window cycle.

CF-H14: consumed offers are counted immediately at **consume**, including queued work. Each settle owns an idempotent release, runs it synchronously before `confirmApplied` (which can itself schedule disposal), and also releases on every early return/throw through `finally`. Disposal marks the transport cut only if unfinished consumed work remains. CF-H15 pins same-tick consume+unmount: remount rejoins, only the baseline ACK exists, and refresh never ran. G1's `heardAt` cursor watermark remains at frame consumption; it was not moved after confirmation.
