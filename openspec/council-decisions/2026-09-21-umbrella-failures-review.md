## Decision

1. **E2E-16 is solved, not masked.** U6 made the token pack's stale-reload row wait for the guest redial before asserting the vault heading (`e2e/gm-two-player-token.pack.spec.ts:277-291`, merge `4139f39e7`, proved three times then, and again today with nothing having touched the spec or its helper since (git log)). Momus's objection stands as a caveat, not a finding: a wait against a race is masking only if the race can outrun the wait, and the row now waits for the event rather than a duration. Residual: the row spends 1.6 of its 4 minutes; if it ever flakes, that is the first place to look.
2. **The genesis clauses (E2E-01/02) pass on main with both arming units present.** U15b's own proof recorded them red because U15a's resolver was not yet merged at that proof's commit (`evidence/u15b-mainproof-20260917.json`); today's measurement is the first with both present, and it is green. The ledger's non-claim ("no ladder run" on U15a and U15b) was correct and is now retired by evidence. What the arm proves is the fixture path: `MEKSTATION_E2E_COMBAT_JOURNAL_AUTHORITY_MODE=enabled` behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_E2E_MODE`, never the production constant, which stays `off` (`src/lib/multiplayer/server/combatJournalAuthorityEnabled.ts`). That distinction is the owner's contract question, below, not a defect.
3. **The rewind rows E2E-40..44 are honestly routed around and cannot be reached inside this change.** The mirror that would journal combat commits returns early when the mode is `off` (`src/lib/multiplayer/server/DurableMatchStore.ts:998`), so the combat stream is never journaled in production (finding #48); the rows are authored verbatim and marked strict `test.fail @until-journal-cutover`. U10 taught the reader the baseline branch name and nothing else; it unblocked none of them. Their owner is `adopt-combat-journal-cutover-and-gm-rewind`, a different change, behind R4.rewind.
4. **E2E-76 may be severable from that gate.** Its letter and body never commit: it opens the preview, checks dialog chrome, focus order and the narrow viewport, presses Escape and asserts the store is unchanged (`e2e/gm-two-player-rewind.pack.spec.ts:311-354`, `specs/e2e-testing/spec.md:321-324`). With U5e/U5f naming the real head, the preview under the fixture arm should answer 200. Inference, not measured: arming the `rewind-pack` group the way U15b armed `authority-recovery` would flip E2E-76 to an unexpected pass while 40..44 stay red. Since E2E-80 closed with U9/U9b, E2E-76 is the only scenario still open on row 22.3.
5. **E2E-19/25 are solved in parts and blocked on one measured gap the loop already owns.** The private reason now travels and is stored GM-only (U5c, U5d); the lobby names the real head (U5e, U5f); the head digest the correction lease demands is the ruled route (U5g, in review; U5h, U5i in flight). 6. **Terminal is honest but two holders are stuck for structural reasons the loop cannot fix.** All 25 open occurrences of the activated package are held (recomputed by Oracle from the admission snapshot). Fifteen sit on deferrals to R4.rewind, R6.B7 and R2.journey. R4.rewind and R3.combat and R3.activation form a dependency cycle written in prose (`roadmap.json` R3.activation close-out gates on R5.E*, which gates on R4.rewind, which depends on R3.combat, which depends on R3.activation), invisible to the validator's `dependsOn` walk; R2.journey depends on R2.camp-8, blocked on a second collaborator the organisation does not have. A status report that says "honestly parked" must also say that.
7. **The acceptance packet is the last contract question, and it should be ruled now, as option (b) with a mechanical definition of "reachable".** Oracle's wording, adopted by the council: every E2E id in the catalogue must exist as an authored row; the excluded set is exactly the authored rows carrying a strict `test.fail` with a named gate tag; the run passes when every non-gated row is green and zero gated rows pass unexpectedly; named gates: `@until-journal-cutover` (owner `adopt-combat-journal-cutover-and-gm-rewind`) and the campaign-genesis production flip (`design-campaign-authority-and-sync` 5.7). This narrows no letter, computes the exclusion set from the tree rather than a receipt, and treats an absent row as a failure, which is what keeps E2E-19/25 honest until U2b lands. The spec delta itself should land after U2b, citing the then-final gated set. Momus's dissent is preserved: fixture-armed evidence proves a path production does not take; the definition above makes that explicit by naming the production flip as a gate rather than hiding it.
8. **Program rule for ticks, to set once: rows tick whole or not at all.** A `tasks.md` row is checked only when every scenario its letter names is proven on main at the tick commit; partial proof is a receipt on the open row; a node may reach complete with rows open as long as each is held (the holders rule already says so). It costs nothing today: 21.2 ticks whole after U2b; 21.1 can tick whole now (below).
9. **Two cheap follow-ups the council recommends.** (a) A narrow successor that re-proves the `authority` group on current main and ticks 21.1, with the key transferred from U3, whose ruling said "hold until the genesis clauses pass": they pass under the fixture arm, so the owner must say whether that satisfies the ruling's intent or whether 21.1 waits for the production flip. 10. **Node close versus change archive are different terminals.** R6.umbrella can close as a node with rows held (its receipt needs a main sha, evidence and cleanup); `harden-gm-two-player-campaign-sessions` cannot archive until R4.rewind, R6.B7 and R2.journey run, because 25.4 is deferred there.
