## Why

Passing isolated CAMP checks does not prove that the player journey is coherent or durable. CAMP-01H closes the program with three independent authority witnesses and a ranked experience reconciliation on one exact SHA.

## What Changes

- Produce distinct custom-save/reload, campaign-readiness, and canonical-combat/post-battle witnesses under one parent run.
- Prove route, API, store, persistence, navigation, reload, command, terminal, and post-battle authority without treating screenshots as persistence.
- Reconcile desktop, mobile, accessibility, feedback, recovery, playability, and enjoyment findings.
- Permit an observation receipt for complete attributable failures, then require focused repair/blocker disposition and a fresh final rerun.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `journey-qc`: Add the final three-witness CAMP-01H authority and experience proof.

## Non-goals

- Making saved custom units combat-capable, weakening the canonical launch boundary, or merging repair implementation into the evidence PR.

## Impact

- Affected contracts: journey QC reports, witness identity, combat authority, experience reconciliation, and H repair gates.
- Delivery remains one later evidence PR with the row's normative `product-pr` subject, capped at 5 files and 300 changed lines; repair-required causes get separate product rows and PRs.
