### Requirement: Saved Custom Unit Campaign Handoff Trust Anchor Journey

Journey QC SHALL include a browser trust anchor that saves a customized canonical BattleMech, reads its server-issued custom-unit id, selects that exact saved design during campaign creation, proves the same source identity and source kind through production wizard submission, campaign roster/root-force server persistence, and cold-reloaded campaign surfaces, then launches canonical units from the same mixed roster into playable production combat and proves command, session, terminal, post-battle, navigation, and cold-reload authority. `camp01-authority-receipt/v1` SHALL provide one strict shared producer/validator whose literal versioned `WAVE_CONTRACTS` table owns each executable wave's command id, ordered per-platform `[executable,...args]` sequence, UTF-8 `JSON.stringify` SHA-256 digest, declared reporter/test/status/fingerprint or complete-observation rules, typed predicates, writer-created isolated run root, allowed artifacts, and producer/validator arguments; caller command text and shell chaining SHALL be rejected. Reviewed-head/exact-main proof SHALL execute from a newly created detached worktree at the verified commit, require matching HEAD/tree plus clean index/tracked state, run every repo-local writer/runner/test from that checkout, baseline all ignored/untracked paths and SHA-256 content after dependency bootstrap, require byte-identical post-run state outside the one exact finalized artifact set, reject extra/temp/path/symlink/junction escapes, and retain commit/tree/manifest-match facts without the local path. Before execution the writer SHALL select only the immutable `commandId` sequence, recompute its literal digest, generate the parent run id, own `command-result.json`, retain only allowlisted command ids/digests rather than raw argv/environment/path/message/stack/reporter payloads, and reject substituted commands or caller-authored, stale, pre-created, or cross-run artifacts. For H the writer SHALL additionally generate exactly three labeled child execution ids, deliver the immutable label/id map only to trusted recorders, require every witness output to carry the common parent run id plus its assigned child id, and reject caller ids, duplicate/unknown labels, missing ids, reused browser contexts/report digests, or cross-label artifacts. Every retained failure/cause fingerprint SHALL be writer-derived `sha256:<64-lowercase-hex>` over bounded allowlisted canonical fields; arbitrary text, paths, secrets, messages, and stacks SHALL fail validation. Every nested H runner SHALL honor the writer-issued `CAMP01_ARTIFACT_DIR`, declare its exact report/artifact mapping, and leave no ignored or untracked output elsewhere; in particular, the UX walkthrough runner SHALL redirect its catalog from the default UX-walkthrough tree into that directory when the contract is present. The required viewport package script SHALL be a single Node orchestrator that executes H's same three Playwright argv arrays sequentially with `shell:false`; chained package values or argv/order drift SHALL fail. Before repair child specs/products, writer-produced PROOF-02 reproduction on fetched exact main SHALL finalize self-digest-free UTF-8 `proof02-reproduction.json`, store its exact-byte SHA-256 only in `command-result.json`, record pre-repair SHA/tree/run, the complete observed aggregate, each historical anchor's passed/failed/missing state, every safe failure fingerprint, and every non-anchor failure for ordinary exit 0 or 1, and require every later consumer to recompute the digest. Fresh status/fingerprint drift SHALL publish for independent triage; incomplete/duplicate reporter output, abnormal exit, or command/provenance drift SHALL reject publication. Before CAMP-00, the zero-command `proof-02-triage` contract SHALL generate writer-owned/digest-bound `proof02-triage.json` from exactly one reviewed disposition per failed/missing anchor and unexpected failure, require set equality with the durable reproduction, verify a merged audit-only PR and exact-head non-author approval, and distinguish required repair, external blocker, ranked lower severity, or reference to a primary observation with the same cause. Each distinct in-scope Critical/Major cause without an external blocker SHALL bind to a separately merged/reviewed/ledgered child OpenSpec whose SHA precedes every reviewed repair-product commit, while already-passing anchors require neither disposition nor repair. CAMP-01H product work SHALL require the recomputed durable triage receipt and every required repair merged or blocker explicit; H/archive completion SHALL require fetched `origin/main` checkout/receipt identity, exact-main `qc:command:browser:quick` with zero failures across its complete observed aggregate, exact passed statuses for the same three historical ids, exactly three witnesses in `session-authority-map.json`—one each for custom save/reload, campaign/Mech Bay/readiness, and canonical combat/post-battle—with the common parent run id, pairwise-distinct writer-issued child execution ids, fresh browser-context ids, isolated labeled artifact subtrees, and non-overlapping durable report digests, and with each witness bound to its own non-empty route/API/store/persistence/navigation/cold-reload report ids plus type-specific identity/command/result facts; shared entity ids MAY prove continuity, but reused child-execution/context/report evidence SHALL fail. Completion also requires machine assertions for accessibility/visibility/feedback/recovery/cognitive-load/playability/enjoyment/positives/categorized findings and all Critical/Major dispositions, a reloaded ten-contract long-campaign checkpoint chain (the REDUCED canonical-combat claim defined below), writer-generated `proof02-repairs.json` containing zero or one verified 17-field repair tuple per required distinct cause, and writer-owned predecessor cleanup records proving merged local wave branches plus registered product/proof worktrees absent while durable evidence remains valid. Spec/product authors MAY be the same, but each exact PR head SHALL have an independently verified non-author approval, EXCEPT under the solo-maintainer reduction (amended 2026-08-09, program-owner decision): in a repository whose collaborator set is API-verified to be exactly its owner, citations MAY carry the sentinel approval tuple `solo-maintainer|<owner-login>`, reducing the approval leg to API-verified owner-authored (and owner-merged, for merged citations) provenance — a REDUCTION of the independent-review claim, not satisfaction of it; receipts produced under the sentinel attest self-reviewed provenance, the sentinel SHALL reject whenever more than one collaborator exists, and the strict clause resumes in full at that moment. #### Scenario: Saved custom identity enters campaign creation

- **GIVEN** the browser customizes and saves a canonical BattleMech
- **WHEN** the custom-unit API returns `<customId>` and campaign creation loads Saved Designs
- **THEN** the roster step SHALL expose a named control for that exact saved design
- **AND** selecting it SHALL create a distinct roster-instance id whose `unitRef` equals `<customId>`
- **AND** the selected draft entry's `unitSource` SHALL equal `custom`
#### Scenario: Campaign and root force persist separate identities

- **WHEN** the campaign is submitted with the saved custom BattleMech through the production wizard path
- **THEN** the journey SHALL observe the wizard's real server PUT and accepted response without invoking a test-only persistence helper
- **AND** browser roster state and server-backed campaign persistence SHALL contain the same roster-instance id, `<customId>` source ref, and `custom` source kind
- **AND** root-force state SHALL contain the roster-instance id
- **AND** no stock-template fallback id or copied construction payload SHALL appear
#### Scenario: Cold reload preserves the handoff through playable combat

- **WHEN** the journey cold reloads and visits the campaign dashboard, Forces, Mech Bay, and mission readiness before launching canonical units from the same mixed roster
- **THEN** each applicable surface SHALL reconcile to the same roster-instance id and `<customId>` source ref, and the blocked custom-row launch attempt SHALL create or mutate no encounter, force, or session
- **AND** the Mech Bay SHALL identify the saved design from custom metadata
- **AND** mission readiness SHALL include the custom roster instance without replacing it with a stock unit
- **AND** mission readiness SHALL name that instance's canonical-combat-unavailable reason and mark the custom row non-launchable
- **AND** the custom row SHALL remain unselected while canonical launch-capable rows remain selectable and SHALL launch through the production UI/API into one server-issued session
- **AND** the tactical surface SHALL expose player commands, accept at least one valid command, restore the same session/authority state after navigation and cold reload, reach a terminal production result, return to the campaign, persist an accepted post-battle consequence, and reproduce that campaign/mission/session/result/consequence state after a final cold reload without altering the saved-custom identity
#### Scenario: Experience evidence and positives are paired with authority proof

- **WHEN** the three independent journeys inspect roster, Mech Bay, readiness, combat command, recovery, and post-battle surfaces at desktop width and 390×844
- **THEN** they SHALL capture named controls, focus behavior, loading/empty/error recovery, feedback, command visibility, decision density, terminology, dead-end freedom, and explicit persona observations for cognitive load, playability, and enjoyment, including positive observations rather than findings alone
- **AND** the reviewed audit reconciliation SHALL separate confirmed defects, inferred risks, coverage gaps, and environment limits; attach severity, reproduction/source trace, screenshots plus route/API/store/persistence/navigation/reload evidence; rank lower severity; and bind every reproducible Critical/Major to its verified focused repair or explicit external blocker
